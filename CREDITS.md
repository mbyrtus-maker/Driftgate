# Model credits

All ship and structure models are CC0 (public domain). They were recoloured into Driftgate's faction palettes and packed into the game file.

- **Kenney Space Kit** and **Kenney Space Station Kit** by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0. These supply the work ships, stations, depots, outposts and station works. The faction fleet carrier and escort are assembled from Kenney parts too (`tools/capitals.py`).
- **Brood** hulls are Driftgate's own, generated by `tools/brood.py`.
- **Lowpoly Spaceships (Jan 2018)** by Quaternius (quaternius.com), CC0. These supply the fighters.

# Sound credits

All recorded sound effects are CC0 or free for any use with no credit required. They were trimmed, levelled and packed into the game file.

- **Sci-Fi Sounds**, **Interface Sounds** and **Digital SFX Set** by Kenney (kenney.nl).
- **SpaceSFX1** by messersm (OpenGameArt), CC0.

`tools/` holds the scripts that build the model pack (`build_models.py`) and the sound pack (`build_snd.py`).
